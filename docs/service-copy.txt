HOUSEKEEPING
--------------------------------------------------

Our caregivers at Elsie J’s Home Care provide light Housekeeping services, with tasks like folding laundry, loading the dishwasher and more to help you keep your home neat, clean and tidy. For a list of Housekeeping services please see below.

Housekeeping Services Offered

Daily Housekeeping Tasks

- Kitchen & Dining Wash and put away dirty dishes, wipe down counters and stovetops, clean up spills immediately, and empty or load the dishwasher.
- Safety & Walkways Clear clutter, cords, and tripping hazards from hallways and primary walking paths.
- Bedrooms Make the bed and tidy personal items.
- Trash & Recycling Empty all wastebaskets and remove kitchen trash, Recycling and Compost if it contains food waste.
- Quick Sweep Sweep high-traffic areas such as kitchen or entry floors.

Weekly Housekeeping Tasks

- Floors Vacuum carpets, rugs, and stairs, and mop hard-surface floors.
- Bathrooms Scrub and sanitize the toilet, bathtub, shower, and sink, and wipe mirrors.
- Laundry & Linens Wash, dry, and fold clothes, and change bedsheets and bath towels.
- Dusting Dust accessible surfaces, tabletops, nightstands, and light fixtures.
- Appliances Wipe down/sanitize the exterior of major appliances and clean the inside of the microwave.

Monthly Housekeeping Tasks

- Deep Fridge & Pantry Discard expired food items and wipe down shelves.
- Windows & Dusting Wash interior windows, clean window sills, and dust baseboards or vents.
- Appliance Detail Clean inside the oven, descale coffee makers, or wash washing machine filters.
- Upholstery & Bedding Vacuum mattress surfaces and launder heavy blankets or pillow protectors.

Keeping your home clean and comfortable doesn’t have to be a burden for you. Our housekeeping services are helpful for seniors, individuals and Veterans. We help your loved ones in maintaining a safe, clean, and comfortable living environment, divided by daily, weekly, and monthly tasks without the hassle.

We understand that every client has different levels of support. While some clients require regular help resetting their homes daily or weekly, some clients prefer support with only the physically demanding chores such as loading the dishwasher or dusting a hard-to-reach area. Elsie J’s Home Care offers personalized care to support independent living while ensuring your loved one is properly cared for.

ALZHEIMER'S & DEMENTIA CARE
--------------------------------------------------

When you have Alzheimer’s or Dementia it doesn’t mean that you are losing your independence. We help clients maintain their dignity by empowering them to continue their regular routines despite the cognitive decline. Alzheimer’s and related dementias are progressive diseases that can worsen over time, and has a significant affect on the cognitive functioning. Many seniors/ individuals with Alzheimer’s also experience Sundowner’s Syndrome. At Elsie J’s we are here to help, our caregivers are trained in Alzheimer’s and Dementia care, so we can better help with our client’s day to day living with these diseases.

Early signs of these diseases can include:

- Memory loss
- Confusion
- Difficulty completing familiar tasks
- Misplacement of items
- Decreased judgment
- Withdrawal from social activities
- Changes in mood or personality

RESPITE CARE
--------------------------------------------------

Respite care services can give you and your family members time to complete their tasks and give them space away from your caregiving needs to relax, recharge and return to you with a clear mind and refreshed energy.

Fun Fact: Did you know that family caregivers and unpaid caregivers are 2 times as likely to experience high levels of stress compared to the average professional caregiver?

When family caregivers try to juggle caregiving with their other responsibilities such as a full-time job or caring for their children or other family members it can have a negative emotional and physical toll on them that you’ll eventually have to deal with.

As a family caregiver, it’s important to remember to take a step back and ensure your own self-care and needs are being met. If you’re struggling to find time for yourself and caring for a loved one with health problems, respite care is designed to help.
